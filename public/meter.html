Currency tilt

Each currency also has its own yield advantage read (is its rate/yield edge widening or narrowing). Tilt = its stable drivers, combined by weight. Green = drivers lean in the currency's favour, red = lean against, amber = mixed / flat. Direction of evidence — not a buy/sell call.

Pair readout

How to read this. It shows the direction of the evidence at your chosen horizon — not the size of the move, and not whether it's already priced in. The rules and weights are fixed and transparent; the tool will not auto-detect regime flips (e.g. yields rising from fiscal stress, or "too-hot" inflation turning risk-off) — use your own judgement to override.

It is never a buy/sell signal. It organises the evidence; what you do with it is your call.

Weights are data-derived (multivariate rolling OLS, 2016→, FRED data) and show only statistically stable drivers per currency. "Commodity / growth cycle" is a literal iron-ore link for AUD/NZD/CAD and a global risk-cycle proxy elsewhere. JPY is honestly a one-driver currency (rate differential). China demand reaches AUD/NZD through iron ore and risk — it isn't a separate arrow here.
