Hi, I'm Vincent. I'm from Singapore.

I grew up in a traditional Chinese family, where the first money lesson was simple: wealth you can't hold is only a promise. So I've held physical gold since I was a kid, and I've spent a lot of the years since asking a slightly obsessive question: if gold just sits there, why does everything else keep moving around it?

FXStrength is where that question ended up. It's a free, live meter that shows how strong the eight major currencies, gold and Bitcoin are right now, across three timeframes. Next to it are plain-English write-ups on why things moved: the data, the central banks, the story the market chose to believe that week.

What it isn't: signals, tips, or anyone telling you what to trade. The meter shows you the picture; the articles show you the reasoning. The decision stays yours.

Where the numbers come from: prices from Yahoo Finance, refreshed about every 15 minutes, so they may be slightly delayed. The method is the same for every currency, and nothing is adjusted by hand.

Why I built it: after 15 years in marketing, I wanted one place that answered "what's actually strong right now, and why?" without a sign-up wall or a sales pitch. I couldn't find one, so I made it.
